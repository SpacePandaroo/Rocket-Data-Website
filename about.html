About RocketLaunchHistory

Hello! I'm SpacePandaroo, a historian with a deep interest in spaceflight and human exploration beyond Earth. My goal is to research, preserve, and share the stories behind the missions, people, and engineering achievements that shape our journey into space.

The Project

This site is designed as an accessible database for students, researchers, and space enthusiasts alike. Whether you're tracking early space race milestones or recent commercial launches, the data is structured to help you explore spaceflight history effortlessly.

Building this database took approximately six months of dedicated data collection, verification, and organization.

Data & Methodology

Data has been carefully compiled from trusted repositories—including Gunter's Space Page, NextSpaceflight, and public mission archives to ensure accuracy and completeness.

Get in Touch & Feedback

Despite thorough review, historical spaceflight data can occasionally vary across archives, and errors may still remain. If you spot a mistake, have feedback, or want to contribute data, feel free to reach out!
